Data Deletion

Delete a habit's data

Applies to the DeenRoutine Android app

In plain English: you can permanently delete any single habit — and its complete history — without deleting your account, directly inside the app. No form to fill out here; the steps below are all it takes.

Steps

  1. Open DeenRoutine and sign in.
  2. Go to the Habits tab.
  3. Tap the habit you want to remove.
  4. Tap Delete in the menu, then confirm.

The deletion happens immediately — there's no waiting period, and it can't be undone.

What gets deleted

  • The habit itself — its title, schedule, and tracking settings.
  • Its full history — every day it was logged as done (or, for an avoidance habit, every logged slip), and the streak calculated from that history.
Everything else stays exactly as it is: your account, your other habits, your quiz results, and your profile are untouched by deleting a single habit.

Want everything gone instead?

To delete your entire account and all associated data — not just one habit — see Delete Your DeenRoutine Account.
