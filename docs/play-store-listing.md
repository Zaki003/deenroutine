## Full description

DeenRoutine helps you build consistency in both faith and daily life — one place instead of juggling a prayer app, a to-do list, and a Qur'an app separately.

Track Salah, Qur'an recitation, and dhikr alongside exercise, reading, or any personal habit — daily, weekly, or on the days you choose. Six tracking styles (simple yes/no, counts, timers, checklists, ratings, or habits you're trying to avoid) mean a habit gets tracked the way it actually works, not forced into one shape.

**Prayer times, always accurate**
Real prayer times for wherever you are, calculated by location — or a city you pick yourself — and refreshed automatically.

**See your day at a glance**
The Barakah Circle shows today's progress in one glance. Streaks are calculated honestly from your actual history, not a counter that resets on a technicality.

**A little daily motivation**
A rotating Qur'anic verse or Hadith greets you each day, plus an Islamic-knowledge quiz whenever you want to test yourself.

**Gentle reminders, your way**
Set a reminder time for any habit — nothing pushy, nothing you didn't ask for.

**No ads. Ever.**
DeenRoutine has no ad network and never sells or shares your data. Usage analytics are entirely optional — off unless you choose to turn them on. Your habit history and prayer data stay yours.

Available in English and Bangla, with light and dark themes.

## Notes

- Describes only what's shipped today — no mention of a future subscription/tip jar, since that isn't built yet and store copy should track the current app, not the roadmap. Revisit this doc when that ships.
- The "no ads, ever" positioning is deliberate, not incidental — see the research behind it: [Pillars](https://www.thepillarsapp.com/) (built directly in response to the Muslim Pro data-selling scandal) leads with "Ad-Free & Privacy-Driven" as its own tagline, and FivePrayer/IslamApp/SalahLock/Muslim Now all foreground "ad-free" as a primary claim — it's the baseline expectation in this app category post-scandal, not a differentiator to undersell.
