In plain English: enter your DeenRoutine email and password below to permanently delete your account and everything in it. This happens immediately and there's no waiting period.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name and email.
  • Your habits — every habit you created, along with its full completion and streak history.
  • Your quiz results — every Islamic-knowledge quiz score and completion date.
  • Your saved preferences — notification and settings records tied to your account.
Cached prayer-time data is not deleted — it's a shared cache keyed by rounded location and date, not linked to your account. See the Privacy Policy for details.
